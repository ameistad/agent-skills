## Impeccable

**Original work:** https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
**Author:** Paul Bakaus
**License:** Apache License 2.0

Adapted and condensed into the files under `references/` and into `SKILL.md`:

- The visitor modes (Persuade, Operate, Read, Experience) and the core design rules.
- The new-work flow: deciding what is already true, the question round, deriving directions from the subject's world, color strategies, the direction contract, and the build and inspection steps.
- The live variant planning method: the identity lock, default versus departure, the six variation axes, and the squint test.
- The playbooks for bolder, quieter, distill, typeset, colorize, layout, animate, delight, and polish.
- The critique structure: separate design and mechanical assessments, heuristic scoring, priority levels, and personas.
- The craft floor: its verification checks and its list of default patterns to refuse.

Changes: the text was rewritten and shortened, Impeccable's engine, detector, browser overlay, image generation, and context files were removed, and the flows were reconnected to this skill's own preview board. No Impeccable code is included.

## Anthropic frontend-design skill

**Original work:** https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design
**Author:** Anthropic, PBC

Adapted into `references/new-work.md` and `references/craft-floor.md`:
